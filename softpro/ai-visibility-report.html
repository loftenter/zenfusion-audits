These systems work by removing hard minerals like calcium and magnesium and replacing them with sodium ions. This process effectively "softens" the water, preventing limescale buildup in pipes and appliances, and improving the feel of water on skin and hair. **Salt-free water conditioners**, as mentioned with the SpringWell Futuresoft, do not actually remove hardness minerals. When choosing a water softener, it's important to consider the **grain capacity**, which indicates how many hardness grains the system can remove before needing to regenerate.
