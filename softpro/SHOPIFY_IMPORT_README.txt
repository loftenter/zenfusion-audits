# Shopify Blog Import — Water Constitutional Right Article

## How to add this to the SoftPro Shopify blog

1. Go to Shopify Admin > Online Store > Blog Posts > Add blog post
2. Title: `Is Access to Clean Drinking Water a Constitutional Right? A Deep Dive`
3. Author: `Craig Phillips` (or create a staff account for Craig)
4. Tags: `water filtration`, `constitutional right`, `clean water`, `lead`, `Sterling v Jackson`
5. In the content editor, click the **<>** (Show HTML) button
6. Open `water-constitutional-right-shopify-export.html` and paste ALL content into the HTML editor
7. Click **<>** again to preview — you should see:
   - Category label "WATER FILTRATION"
   - Article title and date
   - Author card with CP avatar, name, title, YouTube link
   - Dark navy "Key Takeaways" banner
   - Inline Table of Contents
   - Full article body with citations
   - Comparison tables (state + international)
   - Author bio at bottom
   - Related articles
   - 28 numbered references with links
8. Set the blog category to "Water Filtration"
9. Set the published date to September 17, 2026
10. Add the meta description: "A deeply researched examination of whether the U.S. Constitution guarantees a right to clean drinking water, anchored by the 2026 Fifth Circuit ruling in Sterling v. Jackson."
11. Preview and Publish

## File
- `water-constitutional-right-shopify-export.html` — paste this into the Shopify HTML editor
