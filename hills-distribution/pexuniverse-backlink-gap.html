Outreach Strategy Recommendations

Phase 1: Manufacturers (Week 1-2)

  • Identify manufacturer domains in top 50 (Uponor, Viega, Rehau, etc.)
  • Propose co-branded installation guides or product comparison content
  • Offer to feature their products prominently on PEXuniverse site
  • Request reciprocal link to PEXuniverse from their dealer/distributor page

Phase 2: Publications (Week 3-4)

  • Target plumbing/HVAC trade publications in gap list
  • Pitch technical articles: "PEX Installation Best Practices", "PEX vs Copper TCO Analysis"
  • Offer expert quotes for their existing PEX coverage
  • Sponsor content or advertorial with backlink inclusion

Phase 3: Educational & Forums (Month 2)

  • Reach out to trade schools and contractor training programs
  • Offer free PEX installation training materials (with attribution link)
  • Answer technical questions on plumbing forums (signature link)
  • Contribute to community guides and resource pages

Phase 4: Blogs & Directories (Ongoing)

  • Guest post on DIY/home improvement blogs
  • Submit to quality directories (avoid spammy link farms)
  • Collaborate with YouTubers on installation videos
  • Monitor new domains linking to competitors (monthly refresh)
